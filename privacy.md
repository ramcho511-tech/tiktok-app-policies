# Privacy Policy

RamazanAydogduTr Shorts Publisher

Last updated: 2026-08-25

RamazanAydogduTr Shorts Publisher ("the App") is a personal automation tool used to publish the owner's own short-form videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

## Data We Access

The App accesses only the TikTok account explicitly authorized by its owner via TikTok's OAuth login. This includes basic profile information (user ID, display name) and the ability to upload and publish video content, as permitted by the granted scopes.

## Data We Store

The App stores OAuth access and refresh tokens locally, solely to maintain the authorized connection to the owner's own TikTok account. No data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

## Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect data from any TikTok user other than its own authorized account, and does not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling purposes.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Stored tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the App and can be deleted at any time by revoking the App's access from the TikTok account settings.
